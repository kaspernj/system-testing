Default CI checks now retain bounded, payload-free Selenium startup HTTP observations, managed ChromeDriver command receipts/process exits, dist response completion and runtime/export provenance. Failure evidence is printed and saved before cleanup, preserving the primary error if screenshot or quit also fails. Observers detach after startup, and version-probe exits are distinguished from managed-service exits. No timeout, retry, browser lifecycle or public terminal-result contract changes; the underlying intermittent startup and notification stalls remain unproven.
